1. Data Collection & Usage

This app does not collect, track, or upload any personal or device data to external servers. All data remains local to your device.

xerimanage (hereinafter "the App") is designed to respect your privacy. Unlike many modern applications, this App does not include analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party tracking services.

  • No Personal Information: We do not request your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
  • No Diagnostics: We do not collect crash logs, performance metrics, or usage statistics.
  • No Advertising: This App does not display targeted advertisements and does not collect data for ad personalization.

2. Storage & Security

Any data generated by your use of the App (such as game progress or preferences) is stored exclusively in the local application sandbox on your Android device. This data is encrypted and protected by the Android operating system's file system permissions. It is not accessible to other apps and is not transmitted over the network.

3. Permissions

The App does not request unnecessary permissions. If you grant permissions like storage or notifications, they are used strictly for local functionality and are not linked to your identity.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal data at all, the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations are inherently satisfied.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can permanently delete all data associated with the App at any time by uninstalling the application. No data remains on our servers because we do not store any.

6. Changes to This Policy

Since this App does not collect data, our privacy policy is minimal and static. However, we may update this policy to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. Such changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled (or not handled), please contact the developer directly via the "App Info" or "Support" section within the App or on the Google Play Store.
